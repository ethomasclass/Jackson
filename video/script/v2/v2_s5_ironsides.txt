Congress *declared war* in June *1812*. On land, it went badly. The invasion of Canada that the War Hawks were so sure about fell apart almost immediately. Within *two months*, an American general had *surrendered Detroit* with barely a fight. But at sea, one story became famous almost overnight.

In August 1812, the *USS Constitution* met a British warship called the *Guerriere*, a ship notorious for stopping American vessels and impressing their sailors. During the fight, British cannonballs seemed to *bounce off* the Constitution's thick oak hull, and one sailor shouted that her sides were made of *iron*. The Constitution won. By the time she sailed home to cheering crowds in Boston, people had a new name for her: *Old Ironsides*. It stuck for good.
