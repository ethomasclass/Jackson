# King Andrew: music plan (reusing existing cues)

Every cue below already exists. There's no new generation unless we fill a gap.

**Where the files live:**

- **J1:** `ethomasclass/Jackson`, branch `claude/jackson-explainer-videos-oseyfs`, `video/public/music/`. These are the Part One cues.
- **R:** `ethomasclass/Reform-Era`, `video/public/music/`. These are the Fix Everything cues.
- **W:** `ethomasclass/Jackson`, branch `claude/youthful-allen-f14v7e`, `video/public/music/v2/`. These are the War of 1812 cues.

The Bierce repo has no committed music; its Suno files were never pushed.
