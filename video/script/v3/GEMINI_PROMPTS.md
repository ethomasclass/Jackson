# Gemini image prompts: King Andrew

Use these only for moments with no archival image. Real people always come from archival sources, so none of these prompts shows a recognizable real person. Where a real person has to be in the scene, we see them from behind or in shadow.

## How to run them

- **Model:** Gemini image generation (Nano Banana Pro / `gemini-3-pro-image`), at the largest size it offers.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for every image.
- **Style text:** paste the whole prompt, including the **Style** paragraph at the end. Keeping the style text identical is what makes the images look like one series. It's the same style text as Fix Everything, so the channel matches.
- **Sending them back:** send a PNG named as shown (for example `v3_ch05_new_voters.png`). If a result has text, extra fingers, modern objects or a wrong detail, regenerate it rather than fixing it.
- **Mask pass (optional):** some images end with one. When you have an image you like, upload that same image back into Gemini with the mask-pass prompt, and send me both files. It lets me put the coral color and the teal outline on exactly the right figures.
- **If you skip one:** the scene still works. It shows an archival image or a drawn graphic instead. The ones marked **must-have** are where the video is noticeably better with the painting.

**Style** (goes at the end of every prompt below):

> Style: an American oil painting from the 1820s–1830s in the manner of the period's genre painters, such as George Caleb Bingham and William Sidney Mount. Muted earth palette (umber, ochre, slate blue, dull red), warm directional light, visible brushwork, faint craquelure, slightly aged varnish. Every detail must be historically accurate for the stated year and place: clothing, hairstyles, buildings, furniture, lamps and tools. No text of any kind: no letters, words, signs, labels, captions, signatures, dates or watermarks. No frame or border. No recognizable real historical person. Faces, where visible, belong to ordinary anonymous people.
