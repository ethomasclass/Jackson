Andrew Jackson was born in 1767 in the Waxhaws. He married Rachel Donelson Robards in 1791, and again in 1794.

In 1806, he fought a duel. In the War of 1812, they called him Old Hickory. Horseshoe Bend. The Hermitage. The Red Sticks.

In 1824, four Democratic-Republicans ran. Jackson won 99 electoral votes, but he needed 131. The Twelfth Amendment. The Judas of the West. A military chieftain.

John C. Calhoun. Floride Calhoun. John Eaton and Peggy Timberlake. The Petticoat Affair. As chaste as a virgin. The Kitchen Cabinet. The Tariff of Abominations. The Whigs. Andy Veto.

Nicholas Biddle, a wealthy Philadelphian. He nearly went broke on paper IOUs. 56 percent. About 15,000 signatures. About 16,000 Cherokee.

The Cherokee, Creek, Choctaw, Chickasaw and Seminole nations. A writing system created by Sequoyah. Worcester versus Georgia. The decision had "fell still born." The Treaty of New Echota. Alexis de Tocqueville. The early 1800s, in 1838.
