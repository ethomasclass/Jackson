Start with the map. In the early 1800s, most states only let white men who owned property vote. But new western states like Indiana and Illinois let any white man vote.

That's called {expanding suffrage}, the right to vote. By 1828, most states had dropped the property rule.

Once in office, Jackson handed government jobs to his supporters. A senator summed it up: "to the victors belong the spoils." Translation: you win, your friends get hired.

His supporters saw fresh blood. His critics saw a president building a government that answered to him.
